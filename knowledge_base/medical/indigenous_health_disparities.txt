American Indian and Alaska Native Health Disparities (IHS)
Reviewed: 2026-10-24. Every claim cites an entry in knowledge_base/SOURCES.md.

American Indians and Alaska Natives born today have a life expectancy 5.5 years less than the U.S. all races population (73.0 vs 78.5 years). [S2]
Age-adjusted mortality rate ratios, AI/AN (2009-2011) compared with U.S. all races (2010): diseases of the heart 1.1, diabetes mellitus 3.2, cerebrovascular disease (stroke) 1.1. [S2]
The diabetes ratio of 3.2 compares death rates; it is not a ratio of diabetes prevalence. [S2]
Good machine learning practice calls for data that represent the intended patient population, including race and ethnicity. [S4]

Sources:
[S2] Indian Health Service. Disparities fact sheet. October 2019. https://www.ihs.gov/newsroom/factsheets/disparities/

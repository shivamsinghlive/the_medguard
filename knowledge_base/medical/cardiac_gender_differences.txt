Sex Differences in Acute Myocardial Infarction
Reviewed: 2026-10-24. Every claim cites an entry in knowledge_base/SOURCES.md.

An American Heart Association scientific statement reports sex-specific differences in the presentation, pathophysiological mechanisms, and outcomes of acute myocardial infarction. [S3]
Good machine learning practice calls for training and test data that represent the intended patient population, including sex, and for testing that considers important subgroups. [S4]

Sources:
[S3] Mehta LS et al. Acute Myocardial Infarction in Women: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Circulation 2016;133(9):916-947. https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000000351
[S4] FDA, Health Canada, MHRA. Good Machine Learning Practice for Medical Device Development: Guiding Principles. October 2021. https://www.fda.gov/media/153486/download

Adult Cholesterol Classification (NCEP ATP III)
Reviewed: 2026-10-24. Every claim cites an entry in knowledge_base/SOURCES.md.

Total cholesterol in adults is classified as desirable below 200 mg/dL, borderline high at 200-239 mg/dL, and high at 240 mg/dL or above. [S1]
LDL cholesterol of 190 mg/dL or above is classified as very high. [S1]

Sources:
[S1] NCEP/NHLBI. ATP III Guidelines At-A-Glance Quick Desk Reference. June 2001. https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/files/docs/guidelines/atglance.pdf

FDA Context for AI/ML Medical Devices
Reviewed: 2026-10-24. Every claim cites an entry in knowledge_base/SOURCES.md.

FDA, Health Canada, and the UK MHRA published 10 Good Machine Learning Practice guiding principles in October 2021. [S4]
Principle 3: clinical study participants and data sets should be representative of the intended patient population, for example in age, sex, race, and ethnicity. [S4]
Principle 4: training data sets should be independent of test sets, considering patient, data-acquisition, and site sources of dependence. [S4]
Principle 8: test plans consider the intended patient population and important subgroups. [S4]
Principle 9: users should receive information including model performance for appropriate subgroups and known limitations. [S4]
The guiding principles do not state a numeric threshold for performance differences between subgroups. [S4]
In January 2025 FDA issued draft guidance on AI-enabled device software functions (docket FDA-2024-D-4488); it is labelled draft and non-binding. [S5]

Sources:
